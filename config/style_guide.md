# Style guide (injected into every image prompt)

## Positive style
Modern cinematic comic book art, bold confident ink lines, dramatic lighting, rich saturated colours, strong rim light, painterly cel shading, expressive faces, dynamic camera angles, high detail, clean composition.

## Composition rules
- One focal point per panel; characters large in frame; dramatic angles.
- Keep the upper 25% of every panel as empty sky or wall space (speech bubbles go there).
- No studio, artist or brand names anywhere in prompts.

## Negative prompt
text, letters, words, speech bubble, watermark, signature, logo, extra fingers, extra limbs, deformed hands, blurry, lowres, gore, blood, superhero costume, cowl, emblem
