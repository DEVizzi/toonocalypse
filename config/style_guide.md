# Toonocalypse Visual Style Guide (v1)

- **Medium & Linework**: Modern 2D comic-book illustration with crisp, confident ink outlines. Heroes use smooth, rounded outer contours; robotic villains use angular, riveted mechanical contours.
- **Colour & Lighting**: Rich, vibrant, harmonious Saturday-morning comic colours—never muddy and never eye-straining neon. Clean two-tone cel shading with subtle warm rim highlights.
- **Composition & Readability**: Designed for mobile screens. One clear focal point per panel, expressive oversized faces and eyes, bold readable silhouettes, and uncluttered backgrounds with atmospheric depth.
- **Text Rule**: NEVER render speech bubbles, captions, sound effects, labels, watermarks, or signage inside the artwork. All lettering is added later in code.
